1. About this platform

The IT Operations Asset Platform ("the Platform") is an internal system operated by [Your Organization] for managing organization-owned IT assets — laptops, desktops, monitors, phones, printers, networking equipment, software licences and related records — and the requests, approvals, allocations and returns around them.

The Platform is intended for use by [Your Organization] employees, contractors and authorised IT and management staff. It is not a consumer product and is not directed at the general public or at children. This policy explains what personal information the Platform processes and why.

2. Who is responsible

[Your Organization] is the data controller for information processed by the Platform. For any question about this policy or about your personal information, contact:

IT Operations, [Your Organization]
Email: it@example.com

The Platform does not use advertising or analytics cookies. It sets a single session token in your browser after you sign in, solely to keep you logged in.

4. How we use this information

  • To record and manage custody of organization IT assets across offices.
  • To let employees request assets and view the assets and requests associated with them.
  • To route asset requests to IT and management staff for approval, fulfilment or purchase, and to record the outcome.
  • To send transactional notifications about asset requests, approvals, rejections, fulfilments, losses, write-offs and returns (see section 5).
  • To authenticate users, enforce role-based access, and support account recovery.
  • To maintain an audit trail for accountability, troubleshooting and internal reporting.
  • To produce aggregate operational reports (for example, asset counts by office or device type). These reports are about assets, not about individuals.

5. Messaging notifications

A deployment may be configured to send operational notifications over email and/or a messaging channel (for example the WhatsApp Business Platform or Telegram) related to IT asset workflows. Where a messaging channel is enabled:

  • Registered approvers and IT staff receive notifications when an asset request needs approval, is approved or rejected, is fulfilled or marked for purchase, or when an asset is returned, lost or written off.
  • Requesting employees may receive a notification when their own request is fulfilled or rejected.
  • An approver can reply to a request notification — using a quick-reply button or a text reply — to approve or reject a request, and to provide a rejection reason. That response and its sender are recorded against the request.

To provide this, the messaging number of each registered staff member is stored by the Platform and shared with the messaging provider only as needed to deliver these messages. Message content is limited to the operational notifications described above. Messages sent through a third-party messaging provider are also subject to that provider's own privacy policy. Staff who do not wish to use a messaging channel for this purpose can ask IT to remove their number; the corresponding notifications will then not be delivered to them.

6. Legal basis

We process this information on the basis of [Your Organization]'s legitimate interest in managing and safeguarding its IT assets and operating its internal IT function, and, where applicable, to meet record-keeping and audit obligations. Processing is limited to what is necessary for those purposes.

7. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for advertising. Depending on how the deployment is configured, information may be shared only with:

  • The hosting provider that the operator uses to run the Platform and its database.
  • A messaging provider (e.g. Meta Platforms / WhatsApp, or Telegram) — to deliver the messages described in section 5, where enabled.
  • An email provider — to deliver e-mail notifications such as password-reset messages and internal summary digests.
  • [Your Organization] staff who are authorised to operate the Platform, according to their role.

We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect the rights, property or safety of [Your Organization], its staff or others.

8. Data location and transfers

Platform data is stored on infrastructure operated by the hosting provider the operator has chosen, in the region the operator has configured. Notification providers named in section 7 may process message and delivery metadata on their own infrastructure, potentially in other countries, in order to deliver those messages.

9. Data retention

Employee and asset-custody records are retained for as long as the individual is associated with [Your Organization] and for a further period afterwards as needed for audit, reconciliation and historical reporting. Audit and authentication logs are retained for a reasonable period to support security and accountability. Backups are kept on a rolling schedule and then deleted.

10. Security

Access to the Platform requires an individual account and is restricted by role. Traffic to the Platform is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as salted cryptographic hashes. The underlying database and administrative interfaces are not exposed to the public internet and are reachable only through controlled channels. Changes to operational data are recorded in an audit log.

11. Your rights

You may ask to see the personal information the Platform holds about you, to have inaccurate information corrected, or to raise a concern about how your information is handled. Contact IT Operations at the address in section 2. Some information — for example audit and custody history — must be retained for record-keeping and cannot be deleted on request.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it last changed. Material changes will be communicated to staff through normal internal channels.
